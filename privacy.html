Privacy Policy

Radiant Waves · Last updated: August 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Radiant Waves ("we", "us") collects when you visit radiant-waves.com.ng, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal details to read the site. We do collect:

  • Usage metrics — anonymous counts of article views, clicks, and shares, tied to the article URL, not to you personally.
  • Standard server logs — IP address, browser/device type, and pages requested, collected automatically by our hosting provider for security and performance.
  • Location (optional) — if you allow it, your browser may share an approximate location so we can show local weather. You can decline this and the site still works normally.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site running smoothly and, where enabled, to serve ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or generally at aboutads.info.

Third-party links

Radiant Waves aggregates and summarizes news from third-party publishers, and links out to their original articles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites — please review their own policies when you visit them.

Children's privacy

Radiant Waves is a general-audience news site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear cookies at any time in your browser settings, and disable location access from your browser or device settings without affecting your ability to read the site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
